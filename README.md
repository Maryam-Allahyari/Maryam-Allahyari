## Hi, I'm Maryam 👋

I'm a bioinformatician in Toronto with a background in health research.

For four years I managed and analyzed data for the MASHAD cohort, a cardiovascular study of
about 9,000 participants. I kept the participant data in PostgreSQL, derived nutrient patterns
in Python using PCA, compared them between people with and without cardiovascular disease,
and co-authored several papers along the way.

I then did a Master of Bioinformatics at the University of Guelph. My project compared the leaf
microbiomes of herbarium specimens (some from 1938) with field-collected plants of a wild
tomato relative. I built the workflow in Nextflow on the Digital Research Alliance of Canada
HPC (SLURM), with tools for degraded DNA (leeHom, Samtools, Picard, BBMap), Kraken2 for
classification and QIIME2 for diversity analysis.

### Current project
**[Predicting cancer origin](https://github.com/Maryam-Allahyari/Cancer_Analysis)**: can
gene expression and mutations tell us where a metastatic tumour started? Uses data from 570
BC Cancer patients, with scikit-learn, XGBoost and SHAP, and a Docker + Nextflow pipeline.
